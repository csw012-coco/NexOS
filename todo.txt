3. Retire i386 driver legacy surface
   - Status: first cleanup done; driver_i386_stubs.c renamed to driver_i386_legacy.c to reflect its real role.
   - 상태: 1차 정리 완료; 실제 역할에 맞춰 driver_i386_stubs.c를 driver_i386_legacy.c로 이름 변경.
   - Status: second cleanup done; legacy manager/discover/load/file-table aliases were removed.
     The common driver manager now owns driver_discover_root(), driver_load_all(), driver_get_file(), and driver_file_count(),
     while i386 keeps only driver_arch_probe_file()/driver_arch_load_file() for ELF32 .DRV modules.
   - 상태: 2차 정리 완료; legacy manager/discover/load/file-table alias 제거.
     공통 driver manager가 driver_discover_root(), driver_load_all(), driver_get_file(), driver_file_count()를 소유하고,
     i386은 ELF32 .DRV용 driver_arch_probe_file()/driver_arch_load_file() hook만 유지.
   - Status: mechanical split done; ELF32 loader/probe stays in driver_i386_legacy.c,
     while .DRV service symbol exports moved to driver_i386_services.c with a small internal header.
   - 상태: 기계적 분리 완료; ELF32 loader/probe는 driver_i386_legacy.c에 남기고,
     .DRV service symbol export는 작은 internal header와 함께 driver_i386_services.c로 이동.
   - Replace driver_i386_legacy_* aliases with the common driver manager wherever possible.
   - Keep only the i386 ELF32 .DRV loader/hook and truly arch-specific MMIO/IO helpers.
   - i386 driver legacy alias 표면을 줄이고 공통 driver manager로 접기.
   - i386 ELF32 .DRV loader/hook과 진짜 arch-specific MMIO/IO helper만 남기기.
